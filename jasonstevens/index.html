About

What I've been doing

On a film set, the key grip runs the department that builds everything the camera and lights hang from, ride on, or work around: rigging, scaffolding, dollies, cranes and overheads, and the crew that moves them.

I've done that job on some of the largest productions being made, leading the department heads beneath me and the teams they run. It means planning days ahead, moving heavy equipment on a fixed schedule, keeping hundreds of people safe, and never being the reason the work stops.

After two decades of going from one production to the next, I want to bring that to a single team and help build it over time.
